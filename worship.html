Worship

Gather around God's Word, hear the good news of Jesus, and join a congregation that is glad to worship with you.

Sunday Mornings

Worship is at 9:30 AM year-round.

Pilgrim's worship is traditional and approachable. Services are centered on Scripture, preaching, prayer, hymns, and the promises of Jesus.

Music is usually led by piano or organ, depending on the Sunday. Guests are welcome to participate as they are comfortable.
