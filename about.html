About Pilgrim

A WELS Lutheran congregation in Minneapolis where God's Word is taught plainly and people care for one another closely.

Our Church

Rooted in Christ. Warm in community.

Pilgrim Lutheran Church is part of the Wisconsin Evangelical Lutheran Synod and has served the Kingfield neighborhood of Minneapolis for generations.

Our congregation is casual and traditional without feeling formal. We gather around Scripture, worship together every Sunday, and make room for conversation before and after the service.

Welcoming

Visitors, returning members, longtime members, families, and individuals are all welcome at Pilgrim.

Biblical

We teach and confess the Christian faith as a WELS Lutheran congregation, centered on Jesus and the good news of grace.

Neighborly

We are located in the Kingfield neighborhood and want our building, worship, and care for others to serve the community around us.
