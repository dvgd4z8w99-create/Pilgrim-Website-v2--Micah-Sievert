Welcome to Pilgrim

A church with deep roots and room for you.

Pilgrim has served this neighborhood for generations, and the congregation still feels personal. People talk before worship, stay after worship, and make space for new faces with genuine care.

Whether you are returning to church, visiting for the first time, or looking for a congregation in south Minneapolis, you can come as you are and find people who will be glad you came.
